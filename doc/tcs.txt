TCS fast loader of Elite 64 (C64)
=================================

The TCS version of Elite 64 (elite64tcs.d64) has its own fast loader. It
hooks the LOAD vector, opens the file as usual (secondary $60), uploads 1541
code with eight M-W blocks of 32 bytes to $0700-$07ff (CRC $8f5c) and starts
it with M-E $07b5. The data comes from the open file.

Enabled with CONFIG_LOADER_TCS, handled in fl-tcs.c.


Protocol
--------

The handshake is the one of the GMA loaders (doc/gma.txt): the C64 pulls ATN
low and waits for DATA to be released, the drive releases DATA and CLK and
waits for ATN to be released, sends the byte and pulls DATA and CLK low.

The bit pairs are bits 7+6, 5+4, 3+2 and 1+0, CLK first, a set bit pulls the
line low. The first pair starts 27 us after ATN is released, the pairs follow
every 22 us (1541 code: three shifts of a zero page byte and a store per
pair), DATA and CLK are pulled low 8 us after the last pair. The C64 reads
$DD00 32 cycles after it released ATN, then after 23, 20 and 22 cycles.

The stream is the one of GMA85: the sectors of the file including the link
bytes, 256 bytes per sector. A first byte of 0 marks the last sector, the
second byte is the index of its last byte + 1. A first byte of $ff is an
error. The link bytes of a sector that is not the last are ignored by the
C64.
