LCD pinout for HD44780, ST7036 and WS0010 displays, 4-bit mode.
Pins are defined per hardware variant in src/lcd.h.

larsp (MMC2IEC), config larsp-lcd, variant 3:
  RS PB0, RW PB1, E PB2, DB4-DB7 PC4-PC7

sw1 (Shadowolf MMC2IEC 1.x), variant 2:
  RS PB0, RW PB1, E PB2, DB4-DB7 PA4-PA7

sw2 (Shadowolf sd2iec 1.x), variant 5:
  RS PB0, RW PB1, E PB3, DB4-DB7 PC4-PC7

evo2, config evo2-lcd, variant 11:
  RS PB0, RW PB1, E PB2, DB4-DB7 PC4-PC7

Only larsp-lcd, larsp-oled and evo2-lcd have configuration files. For
other boards add the pins to src/lcd.h and set CONFIG_LCD_DISPLAY=y.

WS0010 OLED (Winstar WEH001602A and similar), config larsp-oled:
  Same pins as larsp, CONFIG_LCD_WS0010=y. The module has a 16 pin
  interface in the order of an HD44780 display: 1 VSS, 2 VDD, 3 NC
  (no contrast pin), 4 RS, 5 R/W, 6 E, 7-14 DB0-DB7, 15 and 16 NC
  (no backlight pins). Supply is 5 V (4.8 V to 5.3 V), about 31 mA
  typical, 46.5 mA at most. DB0-DB3 are not used in 4-bit mode.
  There is no contrast setting, the XC command has no effect.
